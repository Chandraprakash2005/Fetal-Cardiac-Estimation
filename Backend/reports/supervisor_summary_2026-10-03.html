Fetal Cardiac Ageing Clock: progress

Prepared for Dr. Muthukumar K A · 3 October 2026 · code state: git 6715f68

Bottom line. Fetal ECG extraction improved substantially: a hybrid of the PINN and a blind-source-separation (BSS) extractor now recovers a usable fetal rhythm in 55 of 60 NInFEA recordings, up from 3. The age model does not yet work. Within NInFEA neither the fetal heart rate nor any of the 12 biomarkers varies with gestational age (GA), so the models can only predict the average. Five decisions from you would unblock the next steps (section 5).

SNR against the scalp lead (ADFECGDB only)

Mean over all windows, after the best scalar gain per window (sign and scale are arbitrary for these extractors): PINN 0.43 dB, cancellation + PINN 0.44 dB, BSS 0.41 dB, hybrid 0.45 dB. In the paper's unit-gain form (both signals z-scored, sign aligned) it is about −1.7 dB, with MSE of about 1.55. Clean records r01 and r08 reach roughly 0.9–1.2 dB; r04, r07 and r10 stay near 0. Allowing a ±40 ms shift only lifts the mean to about 0.6 dB, so timing offset does not explain it. The abdominal fetal waveform differs in shape from the scalp lead, so sample-wise SNR is low even when beat detection is excellent (F1 above 0.9 on r01 and r08), and it does not separate the methods. It is also not comparable with the 14.4 dB in the ICC 2026 paper, whose network was trained to reproduce the scalp signal. Beat-timing metrics are the informative ones here.

The overall correlation comes from separating the two cohorts (21–27 against 38–41 weeks). Within NInFEA the model is worse than predicting the mean.

Why: the inputs do not change with age

  • Doppler fetal heart rate against GA over the 60 recordings: Spearman ρ = −0.07. NInFEA spans only 147–194 days (6.7 weeks).
  • None of the 12 features correlates significantly with GA. I also kept only windows whose extracted rate matches the Doppler rate (166 windows, 51 recordings) to remove noise from wrong-rate windows: still nothing after correcting for 11 tests (strongest: symbolic dynamics ρ = −0.25, p = 0.08).
  • With about 50 recordings only correlations of roughly 0.4 or more would be detectable, so weak effects are not excluded, but they would be too weak to build a predictor on.
  • MSE, SampEn, DFA and PRSA are mostly undefined on 5 s windows, so they could not be tested properly.

5. Decisions I need from you

  2. What the age model can claim. No dataset we have covers 28–37 weeks, and within 21–27 weeks the features show no age signal. Options: report this as a feasibility result, reframe the target, or find data spanning more of pregnancy.
  3. Feature design. The slow-changing dynamic features need long beat series. Computing them per recording instead of per 5 s window would change what a feature vector is.

6. Caveats

  • NInFEA gestational ages and the recording-to-mother table were read from the dataset paper via a summarising tool and have not been checked against the published tables.
  • Doppler peaks trail the ECG R wave by an unknown lag (estimated at about −60 ms with weak concentration), so beat F1 against the Doppler is only approximate; heart-rate agreement does not depend on it.
  • Several mothers have more than one recording; the cross-validation holds all of a mother's recordings out together.
  • No fine-tuning or training touched CinC 2013; the PINN was trained on ADFECGDB only.
